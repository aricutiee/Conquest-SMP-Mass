# Conquest SMP 3.21.1

Includes the AFK display, ten-second rewards, darker sidebar and orbiting NPC icons from 3.21.0, plus TNT changes. TNT minecart explosions cannot damage players. Ordinary primed TNT deals 50% of its normal base explosion damage to players, with armor and other defenses still applied. Mob and terrain damage are unchanged. Cancelled damage remains cancelled. Existing harmless crystal and anchor rules remain unchanged.

320 automated tests passed, including TNT minecart cancellation, TNT reduction, unaffected mob damage, cancelled-event preservation, AFK timing/session logic, orbit bounds and sidebar colors. No live destructive explosion test was performed.
