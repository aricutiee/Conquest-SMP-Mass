# Conquest SMP 3.23.3

Burning normal, tipped and spectral arrows no longer ignite players. Arrow impact damage is unchanged. Existing fire is not extinguished, and lava, fire blocks, melee fire effects and mob ignition remain unchanged. TNT minecart damage remains capped at four hearts.

Includes the original scoreboard colors restored in 3.23.2 while retaining the corrected compact small-caps IP font.
